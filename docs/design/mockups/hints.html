Progressive hints: earned, not handed out

Hints live in the challenge brief panel. They unlock one at a time as wrong attempts pile up, and each tier gives away a little more. They never autocomplete or edit your SQL. You read them and type the query yourself.

Unlock rhythm. Hint 1 unlocks after 2 wrong checks, then one more hint per additional wrong check. "Reveal solution" appears only after all three hints are open, and it asks you to confirm first. Settings has a Hints option: Progressive (default), Always available or Off, for people who want pure challenge mode.

Honest progress, without punishment. A challenge solved after using hints still counts as solved and still keeps your streak going. On its progress segment, a small amber dot marks hints used and an outlined segment marks a revealed solution. The track card offers "Re-solve 3 assisted challenges clean", which gives you a reason to come back.

Extension point. Hints are written per challenge in challenges.toml: one to three tiers, plus an optional skeleton. When each hint unlocks is decided by a HintPolicy trait (by attempts, by time spent, or always available), so the unlock rule can change without touching the challenge content or the UI.
